# 🌸 Daily DSA — Spin Your Topic 🎡

A soft, pastel-themed little corner to make your **DSA journey fun, consistent, and actually doable** 💖
No more “what should I study today?” — just **spin the wheel and start coding**.

---

## ✨ What is this?

**Daily DSA** is a simple yet powerful web app that helps you:

* 🎯 Pick a **random DSA topic** every day
* 📚 Get **study resources + practice problems instantly**
* ✅ Track your **daily progress with a built-in todo list**
* 📈 Build a **consistent coding habit**

All of this… right in your browser. No login. No setup.

---

## 🎡 Features

### 🎲 Spin the Wheel

* Get a **random DSA topic** from 110+ curated topics
* Smart selection avoids recently picked topics
* Smooth, satisfying spin animation ✨

### 📚 Curated Learning

Each topic comes with:

* 📖 Study materials (GFG, guides, etc.)
* 💪 Practice questions (LeetCode & more)

---

### ✅ Daily Study Planner

* Auto-generated **study tasks**
* Add your own custom tasks
* Track progress with a **live progress bar**
* Everything is saved locally 💾

---

### 🔍 Browse All Topics

* Explore all 110+ DSA topics
* Search anything instantly
* Perfect for revision days

---

## 🎯 Why this app?

Because consistency > intensity.

Instead of overwhelming yourself with everything:

> 🌱 *Just focus on one topic a day.*

This app removes decision fatigue and helps you:

* Stay consistent

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

* **HTML5**
* **CSS3 (Custom pastel UI ✨)**
* **Vanilla JavaScript**
* **LocalStorage** (for saving progress)

No frameworks. No dependencies. Just pure frontend.

---

## 💡 How it works

1. Click **"Spin the Wheel"**
2. Get your **topic of the day**
3. Study + solve problems
4. Tick off your tasks ✅
5. Come back tomorrow and repeat 🔁
